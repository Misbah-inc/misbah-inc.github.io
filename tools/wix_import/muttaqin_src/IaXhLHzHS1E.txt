༺《12》༻

The twelfth characteristic of the pious, based on the Sermon of the Pious:

⚜ They possess lean bodies: "wa ajsaduhum nahifah" (and their bodies are lean and slender).

🔸 Imam Sadiq (peace be upon him) said:
"A group of people followed the Commander of the Faithful. He turned to them and asked, 'Who are you?' They replied, 'Your Shia, O Commander of the Faithful.' He said, 'Why do I not see the mark of the Shia upon you?' They asked, 'And what is the mark of the Shia?' He replied, 'Yellowed faces from staying awake [in worship], hollowed bellies from fasting, parched lips from supplication, and the dust of the humble upon them.'"¹
A group set out following the Commander of the Faithful (peace be upon him); His Holiness noticed them and asked, "Who are you?" They said, "Your Shia, O Commander of the Faithful." He said, "Why do I not see the mark of the Shia on your faces?" They asked, "What is the mark of the Shia?" He replied, "Yellowing of the face due to sleeplessness, hollowness of the stomach due to fasting, dryness of the lips due to frequent supplication, and the dust of humility settling upon them."

▪️ God Almighty says: {Their mark is on their faces from the trace of prostration}. And al-Sadooq in the reward of deeds² according to Abu Fazl al-Abbas (AS): that he could see between the eyes the effect of prostration.
Also, the news of the arrival of Al-Ras and Ras Abi al-Fazl al-Abbas (a.s.) to Kufa is documented on the authority of Al-Qasim bin Al-Asbg bin Nabatah - the owner of Amir al-Mu'minin (a.s.) and Hawariyyah - that he said: A man of mine, who was a witness to the killing of Imam al-Hussein (a.s.), who was present at the scene of Karbala, came before us. The reason for that? He said: "No, no, I killed you in Karbala, I am the Companions of Imam al-Hussein Abyad, between the eyes of the effect of prostration, and I came with his head to Ibn Ziyad in Al-Kufah, and that is, I am the one who killed him: Aba al-Fadl al-Abbas (a.s.) who was between the eyes of me, the abundance of worship to God Almighty, the effects of worship and prostration."

Almighty God says:
"Their sign is visible in their faces from the effect of prostration."

Shaykh Sadouq has mentioned in his book Thawab al-Aqmam about Hazrat Abul Fazl Abbas (a.s.) that the effect of prostration could be seen between his two eyes. There is also a story about bringing the holy head of the Holy Prophet to Kufa. Qasim bin Asbagh bin Nabateh - one of the friends and relatives of Amir al-Mu'minin (AS) - says:
A man from Bani Darm tribe who was present in the killing of Imam Hussain (AS) and the incident of Karbala came to us. His face turned black, while before that he was a very white and handsome man. I said to him: I barely recognized you; What caused this color change?
He said with anxiety and distress: "At Karbala, I killed one of Imam Hussein’s (AS) companions—a man upon whose forehead the mark of prostration was clearly visible—and I brought his head to Ibn Ziyad in Kufa."
The person he referred to was Hazrat Abu al-Fadl Abbas (AS); a man upon whose forehead the signs of worship and prostration had become manifest due to his abundant devotion to God.

▪️ *The mark of God’s servants is the humility and submission evident on their countenances;*
*And when the light of day is fully revealed, the day itself bears witness to this truth.*

▪️ *How wondrous are they whom the pre-dawn hours gather in devotion to God;*
*And their gleaming swords bear witness that they are truly free and noble men.*

In these two verses, Sayyid Haydar Hilli describes Imam Hussein (AS) and his companions—foremost among whom stands Hazrat Abu al-Fadl Abbas (AS).
