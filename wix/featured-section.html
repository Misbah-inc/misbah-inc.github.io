Lady Khadijah (p), Ummul Mu'minin

Lady Khadijah al-Kubra (p) was a source of comfort and strength for the Holy Prophet (p), the first believing woman, and a devoted supporter of Islam. She dedicated her wealth and life to the Prophet's mission, became the mother of Lady Fatimah al-Zahra (p), received the greetings of God through Jibril, and held a unique status honored by the Prophet and the Imams of Ahl al-Bayt.
