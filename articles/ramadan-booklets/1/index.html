Ziyārat of Hazrat Khadija (pbuh)

Ziyārat of Hazrat Khadija (pbuh)

Peace be upon you, O wife of the messenger of Allah, the master of all messengers

Peace be upon you, O wife of the seal of the prophets

Peace be upon you, O mother of Fatimah al-Zahra (PBUH)

Peace be upon you, O mother of Hasan and Husayn, the two masters of the youth of all the people of Paradise

Peace on you, O the mother of the pure Imams Peace be on you, O the mother of the believing men Peace be on you, O the first of the believing women Peace be on you, O most sincere of the sincere women Peace be on you, O the chief lady of Sacred Sanctuary and Queen of al-Baṭḥā (Mecca)

Peace be on you, O the first among women to affirm the Messenger of Allah

Peace upon you, O she who fulfilled servitude with its true due of faithfulness

And submitted herself and spent her wealth in the service of the chief of the Prophets

Peace be on you, O the spouse of the beloved of the Lord of the Heaven

The one married to the epitome of the chosen ones. O the daughter of Ibrāhim, the friend (of Allah)

Peace be upon you, O she whom Jibrāīl greeted with peace

and to whom he conveyed peace from Allah, the Majestic Peace be on you, O the protector of the religion of Allah

Peace be on you, O the helper of the Messenger of Allah

Peace be upon you, O the one whose burial was undertaken by the Messenger of Allah and whom he entrusted to the mercy of Allah I bear witness that you are the beloved of Allah and the chosen one of His Ummah And that Allah has placed you in the abode of His mercy in a palace of ruby and pure gold, in the highest ranks of Paradise May Allah send blessings upon you, and His mercy and His blessings
