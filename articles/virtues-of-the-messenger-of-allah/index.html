The Virtues of the Messenger of Allah

Twenty sayings of Imam ‘Ali (peace be upon him) describing the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him and his family)

The Great Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him and his family) and Amir al-Mu’minin ‘Ali (peace be upon him) are, as they themselves declared, a single light in creation. In the blessed month of Rabi’ al-Awwal — the month of the Prophet’s birth — it is fitting to hear him described by the one who stood closest to him of all.

What follows are twenty passages from the luminous words of Amir al-Mu’minin (peace be upon him) in description of the Noble Prophet, drawn from Nahj al-Balagha, Bihar al-Anwar of Allama Majlisi, and Makarim al-Akhlaq and Kitab al-Gharat. They move from his blessed countenance and character, to the raising of Amir al-Mu’minin in his care and the retreat of Hira, to the final farewell beside his pure body.

The Virtues of the Messenger of Allah in the Words of Amir al-Mu’minin

1. The Light of His Mission

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p), describing the excellences of the Great Prophet (p), said:

«اِبْتَعَثَهُ بِالنُّورِ الْمُضِيءِ، وَ الْبُرْهَانِ الْجَلِيِّ، وَ الْمِنْهَاجِ الْبَادِي، وَ الْكِتَابِ الْهَادِي»

"Allah raised him up with a shining light, a manifest proof, a clear path, and a guiding Book."

Source: Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 161

2. The Proof and Guidance He Brought

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p), describing the excellences of the Great Prophet (p), said:

«أَرْسَلَهُ بِحُجَّةٍ كَافِيَةٍ، وَ مَوْعِظَةٍ شَافِيَةٍ، وَ دَعْوَةٍ مُتَلَافِيَةٍ ؛ أَظْهَرَ بِهِ الشَّرَائِعَ الْمَجْهُولَةَ، وَ قَمَعَ بِهِ الْبِدَعَ الْمَدْخُولَةَ، وَ بَيَّنَ بِهِ الْأَحْكَامَ الْمَفْصُولَةَ»

"He sent him with a sufficient proof, a healing admonition, and a call that redeems what was lost. Through him He made manifest the laws that had been unknown, uprooted the corrupt innovations that had crept in, and set forth the rulings that distinguish truth from falsehood."

Source: Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 161

3. “Take Him as Your Model”

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p), describing the excellences of the Great Prophet (p), said:

«فَتَأَسَّ بِنَبِيِّكَ الْأَطْيَبِ الْأَطْهَرِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَ آلِهِ ؛ فَإِنَّ فِيهِ أُسْوَةً لِمَنْ تَأَسَّى، وَ عَزَاءً لِمَنْ تَعَزَّى ؛ وَ أَحَبُّ الْعِبَادِ إِلَى اللهِ الْمُتَأَسِّي بِنَبِيِّهِ وَ الْمُقْتَصُّ لِأَثَرِهِ»

"So take as your model your Prophet, the most wholesome and the most pure (Allah bless him and his family); for in him is a pattern for whoever seeks a pattern, and a source of honour for whoever seeks honour. The most beloved of servants to Allah is the one who takes his Prophet as his model and follows closely in his footsteps."

Source: Makarim al-Akhlaq, vol. 1, p. 8; Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 160

4. A Prayer Upon the Chosen Prophet

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p), invoking blessings upon the Great Prophet (p), said:

«اَللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى نَبِيِّكَ الْمُصْطَفَى ؛ عَيْنِ الْبَرِيَّةِ طِفْلاً، وَ خَيْرِهَا شَابّاً وَ كَهْلاً ؛ أَطْهَرِ الْمُطَهَّرِينَ شِيمَةً، وَ أَجْوَدِ الْمُسْتَمْطَرِينَ دِيمَةً»

"O Allah, send Your blessings upon Your chosen Prophet — the very eye of creation in his childhood, and the best of it in youth and in maturity; purest in nature among the purified, and most generous of all from whom the rain of bounty is sought."

Source: Misbah al-Za'ir, vol. 1, p. 51; Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 105

5. The Best of Families, the Best of Trees

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p), describing the excellences of the Great Prophet (p), said:

«أُسْرَتُهُ خَيْرُ أُسْرَةٍ، وَ شَجَرَتُهُ خَيْرُ شَجَرَةٍ ؛ أَغْصَانُهَا مُعْتَدِلَةٌ، وَ ثِمَارُهَا مُتَهَدِّلَةٌ»

"His family is the best of families, and his tree the best of trees; its branches are finely balanced, and its fruits hang low within the reach of all."

Source: Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 161

6. Most Open-Handed, Noblest in Companionship

It is narrated from Amir al-Mu'minin Ali ibn Abi Talib (p) that he said:

«كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَ آلِهِ أَجْوَدَ النَّاسِ كَفّاً، وَ أَكْرَمَهُمْ عِشْرَةً ؛ مَنْ خَالَطَهُ فَعَرَفَهُ أَحَبَّهُ»

"The Messenger of Allah (pbuh&hf) was the most open-handed of people, and the noblest of them in companionship; whoever mixed with him and came to know him, loved him."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 16, p. 230 — quoting Makarim al-Akhlaq

7. “I Have Never Seen His Like”

In another narration, whenever Amir al-Mu'minin (p) described the Messenger of Allah (pbuh&hf), he would say:

«كَانَ أَجْوَدَ النَّاسِ كَفّاً، وَ أَجْرَأَ النَّاسِ صَدْراً، وَ أَصْدَقَ النَّاسِ لَهْجَةً، وَ أَوْفَاهُمْ ذِمَّةً، وَ أَلْيَنَهُمْ عَرِيكَةً، وَ أَكْرَمَهُمْ عِشْرَةً ؛ مَنْ رَآهُ بَدِيهَةً هَابَهُ، وَ مَنْ خَالَطَهُ فَعَرَفَهُ أَحَبَّهُ ؛ لَمْ أَرَ مِثْلَهُ قَبْلَهُ وَ لَا بَعْدَهُ»

"He was the most open-handed of people, the boldest of them in heart, the most truthful of them in speech, the most faithful of them to a covenant, the gentlest of them in temperament, and the noblest of them in companionship. Whoever saw him unexpectedly was struck with awe of him, and whoever mixed with him and came to know him, loved him. I have never seen his like, neither before him nor after him."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 16, p. 231 — quoting Makarim al-Akhlaq

8. His Stature and His Hair

Ibrahim ibn Muhammad, of the descendants of Ali (p), narrated that whenever Amir al-Mu'minin (p) described the Prophet (pbuh&hf), he would say:

«لَمْ يَكُ بِالطَّوِيلِ الْمُمَّغِطِ، وَ لَا الْقَصِيرِ الْمُتَرَدِّدِ ؛ وَ كَانَ رَبْعَةً مِنَ الْقَوْمِ ؛ وَ لَمْ يَكُ بِالْجَعْدِ الْقَطَطِ وَ لَا السَّبِطِ، كَانَ جَعْداً رَجِلاً»

"He was neither conspicuously tall nor noticeably short; he was of middle stature among his people. His hair was neither tightly curled nor altogether straight — it fell in gentle waves."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 16, p. 193 — quoting Kitab al-Gharat

9. His Blessed Countenance

Amir al-Mu'minin (p), describing the blessed countenance of the Great Prophet (p), said:

«وَ لَمْ يَكُ بِالْمُطَهَّمِ وَ لَا الْمُكَلْثَمِ ؛ وَ كَانَ فِي الْوَجْهِ تَدْوِيرٌ ؛ أَبْيَضُ مُشْرَبٌ، أَدْعَجُ الْعَيْنِ، أَهْدَبُ الْأَشْفَارِ، جَلِيلُ الْمُشَاشِ وَ الْكَتَدِ»

"He was neither heavy of build nor round of face; there was a gentle roundness in his countenance. Fair-skinned with a rosy tinge, deep black and wide of eye, long of lash, with broad shoulder-bones and a full upper back."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 16, p. 193 — quoting Kitab al-Gharat

10. His Gait, and the Seal of Prophethood

Amir al-Mu'minin (p), describing the bearing and gait of the Great Prophet (p), said:

«إِذَا مَشَى تَقَلَّعَ، كَأَنَّمَا يَمْشِي فِي صَبَبٍ ؛ وَ إِذَا الْتَفَتَ الْتَفَتَ مَعاً ؛ بَيْنَ كَتِفَيْهِ خَاتَمُ النُّبُوَّةِ، وَ هُوَ خَاتَمُ النَّبِيِّينَ»

"When he walked he stepped firmly and with vigour, as though descending a slope; and when he turned, he turned with his whole body. Between his shoulders was the Seal of Prophethood — and he is the Seal of the Prophets."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 16, p. 193 — quoting Kitab al-Gharat

11. His Simplicity at the Table

After recounting the qualities of the Great Prophet (p), Amir al-Mu'minin (p) said with longing and love:

«بِأَبِي مَنْ لَمْ يَشْبَعْ ثَلَاثاً مُتَوَالِيَةً مِنْ خُبْزِ بُرٍّ حَتَّى فَارَقَ الدُّنْيَا، وَ لَمْ يَنْخُلْ دَقِيقَهُ»

"May my father be sacrificed for the one who never ate his fill of wheat bread three days in succession until he departed this world, and whose flour was never sifted."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 16, p. 193 — quoting Kitab al-Gharat

12. Raised in His Arms from Childhood

In the Sermon of al-Qasi'a, our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p) recalls his upbringing in the care of the Great Prophet (p):

«وَ قَدْ عَلِمْتُمْ مَوْضِعِي مِنْ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَ آلِهِ بِالْقَرَابَةِ الْقَرِيبَةِ وَ الْمَنْزِلَةِ الْخَصِيصَةِ ؛ وَضَعَنِي فِي حَجْرِهِ وَ أَنَا وَلِيدٌ، يَضُمُّنِي إِلَى صَدْرِهِ، وَ يَكْنُفُنِي فِي فِرَاشِهِ، وَ يُمِسُّنِي جَسَدَهُ، وَ يُشِمُّنِي عَرْفَهُ ؛ وَ كَانَ يَمْضَغُ الشَّيْءَ ثُمَّ يُلْقِمُنِيهِ ؛ وَ مَا وَجَدَ لِي كَذْبَةً فِي قَوْلٍ، وَ لَا خَطْلَةً فِي فِعْلٍ»

"You know well my standing with the Messenger of Allah (pbuh&hf) — the closeness of kinship and the special rank. He placed me in his lap when I was a small child, pressing me to his chest, sheltering me in his bed, letting me feel his body and breathe his fragrance. He would soften a morsel and then place it in my mouth. He never found a lie in my speech, nor a fault in my deed."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 38, p. 320; Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 192 (al-Qasi'a)

13. Guided by the Greatest of Angels

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p), describing the divine nurturing of the Great Prophet (p), said:

«وَ لَقَدْ قَرَنَ اللهُ بِهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَ آلِهِ مِنْ لَدُنْ كَانَ فَطِيماً أَعْظَمَ مَلَكٍ مِنْ مَلَائِكَتِهِ ؛ يَسْلُكُ بِهِ طَرِيقَ الْمَكَارِمِ، وَ مَحَاسِنَ أَخْلَاقِ الْعَالَمِ ؛ لَيْلَهُ وَ نَهَارَهُ»

"Indeed, from the time he was weaned, Allah joined to him the greatest of His angels, to lead him along the path of noble qualities and the finest character of the world, by night and by day."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 38, p. 320; Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 192 (al-Qasi'a)

14. “As a Young Camel Follows Its Mother”

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p) tells of his constant companionship with the Great Prophet (p):

«وَ لَقَدْ كُنْتُ أَتَّبِعُهُ اتِّبَاعَ الْفَصِيلِ أَثَرَ أُمِّهِ ؛ يَرْفَعُ لِي فِي كُلِّ يَوْمٍ عَلَماً مِنْ أَخْلَاقِهِ، وَ يَأْمُرُنِي بِالِاقْتِدَاءِ بِهِ»

"I would follow him as a young camel follows the tracks of its mother. Each day he would raise up for me a banner of his character, and command me to follow it."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 38, p. 320; Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 192 (al-Qasi'a)

15. The Solitude of Hira

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p) recalls the solitude of Islam's earliest days and the retreat of Hira:

«وَ لَقَدْ كَانَ يُجَاوِرُ فِي كُلِّ سَنَةٍ بِحِرَاءَ فَأَرَاهُ وَ لَا يَرَاهُ غَيْرِي ؛ وَ لَمْ يَجْمَعْ بَيْتٌ وَاحِدٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ فِي الْإِسْلَامِ غَيْرَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَ آلِهِ وَ خَدِيجَةَ وَ أَنَا ثَالِثُهُمَا ؛ أَرَى نُورَ الْوَحْيِ وَ الرِّسَالَةِ، وَ أَشَمُّ رِيحَ النُّبُوَّةِ»

"Every year he would withdraw to Hira, where I saw him and no one else saw him. In those days Islam had not gathered in a single household save that of the Messenger of Allah (pbuh&hf) and Khadija — and I was the third of them. I beheld the light of revelation and messengership, and I breathed the fragrance of prophethood."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 38, p. 320; Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 192 (al-Qasi'a)

16. The Wailing of Satan at the Descent of Revelation

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p) relates the moment revelation descended upon the Great Prophet (p):

«وَ لَقَدْ سَمِعْتُ رَنَّةَ الشَّيْطَانِ حِينَ نَزَلَ الْوَحْيُ عَلَيْهِ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَ آلِهِ ؛ فَقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، مَا هَذِهِ الرَّنَّةُ؟ فَقَالَ: هَذَا الشَّيْطَانُ قَدْ أَيِسَ مِنْ عِبَادَتِهِ ؛ إِنَّكَ تَسْمَعُ مَا أَسْمَعُ، وَ تَرَى مَا أَرَى، إِلَّا أَنَّكَ لَسْتَ بِنَبِيٍّ، وَ لَكِنَّكَ وَزِيرٌ، وَ إِنَّكَ لَعَلَى خَيْرٍ»

"Truly I heard the wail of Satan when the revelation came down upon him (pbuh&hf). I said: 'O Messenger of Allah, what is this wailing?' He said: 'This is Satan, who has despaired of being worshipped. You hear what I hear and you see what I see, except that you are not a prophet — rather you are a vizier, and you are indeed upon good.'"

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 38, pp. 320–321; Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 192 (al-Qasi'a)

17. His Household and His Justice

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p), describing the household and the way of the Great Prophet (p), said:

«عِتْرَتُهُ خَيْرُ الْعِتَرِ، وَ أُسْرَتُهُ خَيْرُ الْأُسَرِ، وَ شَجَرَتُهُ خَيْرُ الشَّجَرِ ؛ نَبَتَتْ فِي حَرَمٍ، وَ بَسَقَتْ فِي كَرَمٍ ؛ لَهَا فُرُوعٌ طِوَالٌ وَ ثَمَرٌ لَا يُنَالُ ؛ فَهُوَ إِمَامُ مَنِ اتَّقَى، وَ بَصِيرَةُ مَنِ اهْتَدَى ؛ سِيرَتُهُ الْقَصْدُ، وَ سُنَّتُهُ الرُّشْدُ، وَ كَلَامُهُ الْفَصْلُ، وَ حُكْمُهُ الْعَدْلُ»

"His kindred are the best of kindred, his family the best of families, and his tree the best of trees. It took root in a sanctuary and rose tall in nobility; it has lofty branches and fruit beyond reach. He is the leader of every God-fearing soul and the insight of every guided one. His conduct is moderation, his way is right guidance, his speech is the decisive word, and his judgement is justice."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 16, p. 379; Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 94

18. His Abode, His Speech, His Silence

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p), telling of the blessings brought by the Great Prophet (p), said:

«مُسْتَقَرُّهُ خَيْرُ مُسْتَقَرٍّ، وَ مَنْبِتُهُ أَشْرَفُ مَنْبِتٍ ؛ فِي مَعَادِنِ الْكَرَامَةِ وَ مَمَاهِدِ السَّلَامَةِ ؛ قَدْ صُرِفَتْ نَحْوَهُ أَفْئِدَةُ الْأَبْرَارِ، وَ ثُنِيَتْ إِلَيْهِ أَزِمَّةُ الْأَبْصَارِ ؛ دَفَنَ بِهِ الضَّغَائِنَ، وَ أَطْفَأَ بِهِ النَّوَائِرَ ؛ أَلَّفَ بِهِ إِخْوَاناً، وَ فَرَّقَ بِهِ أَقْرَاناً ؛ أَعَزَّ بِهِ الذِّلَّةَ، وَ أَذَلَّ بِهِ الْعِزَّةَ ؛ كَلَامُهُ بَيَانٌ، وَ صَمْتُهُ لِسَانٌ»

"His abode is the best of abodes and his origin the noblest of origins — among the mines of nobility and the cradles of safety. The hearts of the righteous have been turned toward him, and the reins of every gaze bent his way. Through him, God removed hatred and put out the fires of conflict; Through him, He brought people together as brothers and separated those who opposed the truth.; Through him, He gave honor to the humble and humbled the proud. His words were clear and meaningful, and even his silence spoke."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 16, p. 379; Nahj al-Balagha, Sermon 96

19. Chosen from the Tree of the Prophets

Our master Amir al-Mu'minin (p), telling of the chosenness of the Great Prophet (p), said:

«اخْتَارَهُ مِنْ شَجَرَةِ الْأَنْبِيَاءِ، وَ مِشْكَاةِ الضِّيَاءِ، وَ ذُؤَابَةِ الْعَلْيَاءِ، وَ سُرَّةِ الْبَطْحَاءِ، وَ مَصَابِيحِ الظُّلْمَةِ، وَ يَنَابِيعِ الْحِكْمَةِ»

"He chose him from the tree of the prophets, from the lamp-niche of light, from the loftiest summit of eminence, from the very heart of Batha, from the lamps that dispel darkness, and from the wellsprings of wisdom."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 16, p. 381; Nahj al-Balagha

20. The Final Farewell

When Amir al-Mu'minin (p) had finished the washing of the pure body of the Messenger of Allah (pbuh&hf), he drew back the cloth from his blessed face and said:

«بِأَبِي أَنْتَ وَ أُمِّي، طِبْتَ حَيّاً وَ طِبْتَ مَيِّتاً ؛ انْقَطَعَ بِمَوْتِكَ مَا لَمْ يَنْقَطِعْ بِمَوْتِ أَحَدٍ مِمَّنْ سِوَاكَ مِنَ النُّبُوَّةِ وَ الْإِنْبَاءِ ؛ خُصِّصْتَ حَتَّى صِرْتَ مُسَلِّياً عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ، وَ عَمَّمْتَ حَتَّى صَارَ النَّاسُ فِيكَ سَوَاءً ؛ وَ لَوْ لَا أَنَّكَ أَمَرْتَ بِالصَّبْرِ وَ نَهَيْتَ عَنِ الْجَزَعِ لَأَنْفَدْنَا عَلَيْكَ الشُّئُونَ ؛ بِأَبِي أَنْتَ وَ أُمِّي، اذْكُرْنَا عِنْدَ رَبِّكَ وَ اجْعَلْنَا مِنْ هَمِّكَ»

"May my father and my mother be sacrificed for you. You were pure in life and pure in death. With your passing there was severed what the death of no one else has severed: prophethood and divine tidings. You were so singled out that you became a consolation for every other loss, and so universal that all people are equal in grieving you. Were it not that you yourself commanded patience and forbade despair, we would have exhausted the very wellsprings of our tears over you. May my father and my mother be sacrificed for you — remember us with your Lord, and keep us in your care."

Source: Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 22, p. 526

May Allah’s peace and blessings be upon Muhammad and his purified family, and may we be granted to walk in the light of his example.
